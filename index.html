01 · Getting started

Import from QuickMock or any share sheet

Choose a screenshot in the app

Open QuickMock and tap Choose Screenshot or New Mock. Apple’s picker shares only the item you choose; QuickMock does not browse your full Photo Library.

Add QuickMock to the Share Sheet

  1. Open a PNG, JPEG, or HEIF image in Photos or Files.
  2. Tap Share and scroll through the app row.
  3. Choose More, then select QuickMock. Add it to Favorites if you want faster access later.

The Share Extension accepts up to four images. If you select two to four images, QuickMock attempts to stitch them in their supplied order. Animated and multipage images are not supported.

02 · Frames and layout

Fit the frame to your screenshot

QuickMock did not select a frame automatically

QuickMock avoids guessing when an image is cropped or does not reliably match a known screenshot aperture. Tap Frame to search by model or dimensions and choose a procedural fit manually. Choose None when you want no device shell.

Portrait and landscape

Use the compact orientation control on the canvas to rotate the device shell. QuickMock keeps the source screenshot upright and recalculates its screen fit.

Smart Screen Fill

  • Smart chooses a balanced treatment that avoids an aggressive crop.
  • Fit preserves the complete image and may leave unused screen space.
  • Fill fills the aperture and may crop an edge.
  • Ambient keeps the full screenshot visible over a soft, color-matched backdrop.

Frame accuracy

QuickMock matches screenshot dimensions and aspect ratios, then draws original generic shells with continuous corners and generic camera cutouts. Search aliases do not mean the frame is official manufacturer artwork or an exact exterior-hardware replica.

03 · Export

Save exactly what you designed

Save to Photos

Tap Export, select QuickMock or Clean, and choose Save to Photos. The first save may request add-only Photos access. If access was denied, open Settings → Apps → QuickMock → Photos, allow adding photos, and try again. QuickMock cannot browse your library.

Save to Files or another app

Choose Share or Save to Files to open Apple’s share sheet. Available destinations depend on the apps and services installed on your iPhone.

Transparent output

In Style, choose Transparent, then export a PNG. The editor checkerboard is only a transparency guide and is not included in the file. Some share destinations flatten transparent images; use Save to Files when you need to preserve alpha with the least ambiguity.

Canvas sizes

QuickMock includes Social Portrait 4:5 (1440 × 1800), Square 1:1 (1440 × 1440), Story 9:16 (1440 × 2560), Landscape 16:9 (1920 × 1080), and App Store 6.9-inch portrait (1320 × 2868) and landscape (2868 × 1320) presets. The spacing control changes the breathing room without changing the chosen output dimensions. App Store presets require an opaque background.

QuickMock branding

In QuickMock 1.0, select Clean to remove the optional “QuickMock” mark at no charge, or select QuickMock to keep it.

04 · Privacy tools

Redact before you share

Open Redact, select Blur or Pixelate, and swipe over anything sensitive. You can reposition or remove redaction regions before exporting.

The exported result is flattened, so the hidden source pixels are not stored as an editable layer inside that output. Your local editable project can still retain the original screenshot. Delete the Recent Mock if you no longer want QuickMock to keep the original locally.

Always inspect the final export at full size. Redaction is a visual tool; you are responsible for confirming every private area is completely covered before sharing.

05 · Long screenshots

Get a clean stitch

Select two to four sequential screenshots in top-to-bottom order. Each adjacent image needs a clear, unchanged overlap so QuickMock can align the shared pixels and remove the duplicate region.

Why stitching can fail

  • there is too little overlap;
  • the screenshots have different widths or scales;
  • a sticky header, animation, video, or timestamp changed in the overlap;
  • the images were selected out of order; or
  • the combined image exceeds safe processing limits.

Capture again with a larger stable overlap and select the images in reading order. QuickMock rejects an ambiguous match rather than silently producing a misleading result.

06 · Projects and storage

Manage Recent Mocks

Recent Mocks stores editable projects locally so you can reopen them. Long-press a project and choose Delete, or open the Recent Mocks menu and choose Delete All.

QuickMock 1.0 keeps up to 100 editable projects and builds its device frames offline. Removing QuickMock also removes its embedded Share Extension and their shared app data according to iOS behavior. QuickMock has no account or server-side image library to clear.

07 · Common questions

Answers at a glance

Does QuickMock upload my screenshots?

No. Import validation, frame fitting, styles, redaction, stitching, and final rendering happen on your device. The current release’s optional online frame registry is disabled.

Why does the exported image look different in another app?

Some destinations compress images, change color presentation, resize them, or flatten transparency. Compare the file saved to Photos or Files with the destination’s copy, and use Files when preserving an exact PNG is important.

Can QuickMock edit videos?

No. This release supports still PNG, JPEG, and HEIF image workflows. Video, animated, and multipage export are not currently supported.

Is iPad supported?

The current app is optimized and distributed for iPhone. Tablet and Watch options in the frame picker are mockup-frame categories, not a statement that the QuickMock app itself runs on those devices.

Do I need QuickMock PRO?

No. In QuickMock 1.0, all tools, backgrounds, stitching, shadow controls, clean exports, and optional-brand removal are included free.

What should I include in a support request?

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, QuickMock version and build, whether you started in the app or Share Sheet, the steps you took, and the exact message shown. If an image is necessary, crop or redact private information first.
