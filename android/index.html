How do I import a screenshot?

Choose Screenshot opens Android’s system picker. Select an image to open the editor. You can also share a supported PNG, JPEG, or HEIF still image from Photos, Gallery, Files, or a compatible app and choose QuickMock. The sending app must provide an actual image attachment; text, links, web pages, and videos are not screenshot imports.

How do frames and Screen Fill work?

Open Frame to search generic phone, tablet, watch, or custom fits, or choose No Frame. QuickMock uses original procedural geometry rather than official manufacturer artwork. Automatic detection is conservative; choose a frame manually for cropped or unusual images. Rotating the frame changes its shell orientation while keeping your source image upright.

Smart balances crop risk. Fit shows the complete image, Fill covers the screen aperture, and Ambient keeps the full image visible over a coordinated backdrop. All frame generation and image analysis happen locally, including when offline.

Can I export with a transparent background?

Yes. Choose Transparent in Style, then export PNG. Clear and partially transparent pixels are preserved. The editor’s checkerboard is not included. App Store presets require an opaque background; choose a solid or gradient background for those exact formats.

How do I hide private details?

Open Redact, select Blur or Pixelate, and draw over the sensitive area. A labeled center-region control is available as an alternative to drawing. Adjust strength, inspect the preview, and export. Redaction is flattened into the finished image, but the editable local project may still retain its original source. Delete the project if you no longer want that original stored.

How do I save or share the result?

Open Export, choose Clean or QuickMock branding, and select PNG or JPEG where supported. Save to Photos adds the result to Pictures/QuickMock using Android MediaStore. Save to Files opens the system file chooser. Share opens Android’s share sheet. QuickMock does not need broad photo-library access to add an exported image.

Which sizes can I create?

Portrait 4:5 (1440 × 1800), Square 1:1 (1440 × 1440), Story 9:16 (1440 × 2560), Landscape 16:9 (1920 × 1080), App Store Portrait (1320 × 2868), and App Store Landscape (2868 × 1320). Spacing changes the breathing room around the image without changing the output dimensions.

Why can’t these screenshots be stitched?

Choose two to four screenshots in top-to-bottom order with clear, unchanged overlapping content. Reorder the selection if needed before stitching. Different widths, animated content, sticky elements, or too little overlap can prevent a reliable match. Keep the selection within the displayed memory and size limits.

Do I need a purchase, account, or internet connection?

No. All QuickMock 1.0 editing, stitching, backgrounds, shadows, and high-resolution exports are available without a purchase or account. The optional QuickMock mark can be removed at no charge. Editing and procedural frames work offline. A cloud-backed image may need its original downloaded by Android’s picker before import; external support pages and email need their own connections.

How do I delete my data?

Delete an individual item from Recent Mocks or choose Delete All. This removes local project files, originals, and previews. Small preferences remain until reset or uninstall. Exports already saved to Photos, Files, or other apps remain at those destinations. QuickMock has no cloud account or server copy of your images.

Can I use QuickMock on a tablet?

Yes. QuickMock adapts its editor and recent-project layout to larger Android windows. Your chosen output preset controls export dimensions independently of your device’s screen size.
